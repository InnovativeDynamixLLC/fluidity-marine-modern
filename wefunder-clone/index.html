The Pitch

Fluidity Marine is building a future where boats steer without rudders — using seven synchronized water jets at the transom, controlled 2,000 times per second. The Active Boundary Layer Thrust Vectoring (TVC) system replaces the 2,000-year-old rudder entirely, eliminating drag and enabling zero-appendage steering. We've filed 5 patents, completed 200+ CFD simulations, and are building our first prototype vessel.
